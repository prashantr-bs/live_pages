What the read-only DOM-layer POC (ios_app_patcher · prashantr/webview-dom-layer-poc) supports, for the 37 rules that rule_config.json lists for iOS. The production dylib detects nothing in WebViews today. Measured on an Angular + Ionic + Capacitor test app: local on an iPad Air (iPadOS 26.5), and two prod runs on an iPhone 16 (iOS 18): #1, WCAG 2.1 AA, with AI and #2, WCAG 2.2 AAA.
